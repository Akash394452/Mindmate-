# MindMate 🧠

> **An AI-Powered Integrated Student Wellness Ecosystem**

MindMate is a unified platform designed to address the primary challenges faced by college students: **study stress, career confusion, and relationship problems** through AI personalization and expert human support.

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- **AI Personalization & Matching:** Assesses student goals and matches them with suitable counsellors, mentors, or resources.
- **360° Support System:** Covers Career Guidance, Study-Stress Management, Relationship Support, and Mental Wellbeing.
- **Hybrid Support:** Smart AI-assisted recommendations backed by qualified human professionals and peer mentors.
- **Accessibility:** Regional language support and affordable access.

---

## 💼 Business Model
- **B2B Model:** Direct college partnerships for bulk student access.
- **B2C Model:** Freemium structure for individual students (Free Basic Plan & Pro Subscription).
